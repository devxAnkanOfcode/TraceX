THE PROBLEM

Crypto fraud moves faster than manual investigation.

TRACE//X is a prototype for turning a victim's first piece of evidence — a suspect wallet or transaction hash — into a structured blockchain investigation.

The interface is designed to make complex on-chain movement understandable during a live hackathon demo, while leaving clear seams for real blockchain APIs, indexing services, entity datasets and an AI scoring layer.
